Aegis Ledger

A permissioned blockchain concept for national defense supply chains and access records.

Concept Draft · November 2026
Read the whitepaper (PDF)

Overview

Aegis Ledger is a permissioned blockchain used as an immutable national ledger. It records critical defense supply-chain events and system access logs. Authorized parties share one consistent history. No single organization controls the record.

Problem

Defense supply chains and sensitive systems rely on separate databases. A single compromised server can alter records, hide shipments, or erase access logs. Cross-agency visibility is slow and incomplete.

Solution

Each critical event, such as a component leaving a factory, a clearance granted, or a system login, is written once. Independent nodes operated by different agencies or trusted partners must agree before the entry is final. Once written, it cannot be changed or deleted.

Properties

  • No single point of failure or control.
  • Full history visible to authorized parties in real time.
  • Tamper-evident by design.
  • Clear attribution of every change.
  • Operates alongside existing infrastructure.

Status

A concept in design. No system has been built, tested, or deployed.
